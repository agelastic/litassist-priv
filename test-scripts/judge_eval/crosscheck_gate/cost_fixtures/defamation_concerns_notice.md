# Concerns Notice - Defamation

**Matter:** Dr Helen Sato - publication by Coastline Community News
**Jurisdiction:** New South Wales
**Prepared:** for the aggrieved person

CONCERNS NOTICE UNDER THE DEFAMATION ACT 2005 (NSW)

To: The Editor, Coastline Community News Pty Ltd

## Re: Article "Local clinic under cloud" published 3 May 2025

1. We act for Dr Helen Sato, a general practitioner who has practised at the
   Coastline Medical Centre for eleven years.

2. On 3 May 2025 you published, in print and online, an article headed "Local
   clinic under cloud" which conveyed the following defamatory imputations of our
   client:
   (a) that she fraudulently overcharged Medicare;
   (b) that she is incompetent and has endangered patients; and
   (c) that she is under criminal investigation.

3. Each imputation is false. Our client has never been the subject of any Medicare
   compliance finding, any disciplinary proceeding, or any criminal
   investigation. The article relied on an anonymous "former patient" and no
   attempt was made to contact our client before publication.

4. The publication is defamatory of our client and has caused, and is likely to
   continue to cause, serious harm to her reputation, as required by s 10A of the
   Defamation Act 2005 (NSW). The matter was published to a large audience: the
   online article has been shared widely and the imputations concern her
   professional integrity.

5. This is a concerns notice for the purposes of Part 3 of the Act. Our client
   requires, within 28 days, a published apology in equal prominence, removal of
   the online article, and an offer to make amends including reasonable
   compensation and costs.

6. If you do not make a reasonable offer to make amends, our client reserves the
   right to commence proceedings in the Supreme Court of New South Wales, in which
   she will rely on this notice.

Please direct all correspondence to this office.
