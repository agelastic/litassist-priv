# Letter of Demand - Residential Tenancy Bond

**Matter:** Re tenancy at 14 Marlin Street, Coorparoo QLD
**Jurisdiction:** Queensland
**Prepared:** for the tenant

OPEN LETTER

Dear Sir/Madam

## Re: Return of rental bond - 14 Marlin Street, Coorparoo

1. We act for Mr Thomas Reedy, the former tenant of the above premises under a
   residential tenancy agreement that ended on 30 March 2025.

2. A rental bond of $1,840 was lodged with the Residential Tenancies Authority. On
   vacating, our client left the premises clean and undamaged, fair wear and tear
   excepted, and returned all keys.

3. The lessor has refused to release the bond, asserting $1,200 for "carpet
   replacement" and $640 for "garden restoration". Our client disputes both. The
   carpet was already worn at the start of the tenancy, as recorded in the entry
   condition report dated 1 April 2023, and the lessor provided no quote, invoice
   or photographs. A lessor cannot recover for fair wear and tear: see the
   Residential Tenancies and Rooming Accommodation Act 2008 (Qld) and the
   obligation on a lessor to substantiate any claim against the bond.

4. The entry condition report and our client's photographs taken on 29 March 2025
   show the premises in materially the same condition as at the start of the
   tenancy.

5. Our client demands release of the full bond of $1,840 within 14 days. If the
   bond is not released, our client will apply to the Queensland Civil and
   Administrative Tribunal for an order, and will seek costs.

Please direct all correspondence to this office.
